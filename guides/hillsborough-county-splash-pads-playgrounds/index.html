Hillsborough County Splash Pads with Playgrounds: 3 Official Options for 2026

Sometimes the water feature is the plan, and sometimes the playground has to rescue the outing. Hillsborough County currently lists all three places below as splash-pad locations, and each location page also documents a playground.

This is not a complete list, and these parks have not been personally tested. The comparison uses only current Hillsborough County pages. Anything those pages do not confirm stays unknown.

Shared County schedule and rules

Hillsborough County publishes splash-pad hours of 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. from April through October and 9 a.m. to dusk from November through March. The County warns that maintenance and repairs can cause downtime, says there is no lifeguard, and requires adult supervision for children under 16. It also recommends water shoes because surfaces may be hot and slippery.

1. All Persons Rotary Park

Address: 800 S. Parsons Road, Brandon, FL 33511.

The County describes this as a non-staffed, 8.09-acre park. Its amenity fields list a universal playground, splash pad, public parking, restrooms, picnic shelters, and pavilions. The fee field says free, and park hours are sunrise to sunset.

Unknown on the current page: Live splash-pad operation · Shade · Equipment age range · Current condition · Whether every feature is accessible · Parking capacity · Restroom operating condition

Before you go: Check the County location page for a repair notice. Treat “universal playground” as the County's amenity label, not a guarantee about every park or splash-pad feature.

Source: All Persons Rotary Park, Hillsborough County

2. Branchton Park

Address: 15504 Morris Bridge Road, Thonotosassa, FL 33592.

The County says the renovated park reopened in January 2025 with a playground, splash pad, shelters, and basketball courts. Its park hours are sunrise to sunset.

Unknown on the current page: Fee · Parking · Restrooms · Shade · Splash-pad design · Accessibility details · Live water operation

Before you go: In September, use the County's 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. splash-pad schedule while also respecting the park's sunrise-to-sunset hours. Verify same-day operation and do not count amenities described for later renovation phases as available.

Source: Branchton Park, Hillsborough County

3. Northdale Park & Recreation Center

Address: 15510 Hooting Owls Place, Tampa, FL 33624.

The County's outdoor-amenity list includes a splash pad, playground, basketball courts, fitness equipment, picnic shelters, tennis courts, and a paved trail. The fee field says free. The center is listed as open Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. and Saturday and Sunday from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.; outdoor areas are listed from sunrise to 9 p.m.

Unknown on the current page: Live splash-pad operation · Parking · Restrooms · Shade · Equipment age range · Feature-specific accessibility · Whether optional programs, rentals, or indoor activities are free

Before you go: Do not treat the outdoor-area closing time as splash-pad hours. In September, the separate County splash-pad schedule ends at 7 p.m. Check the location page for downtime and any activity-specific requirements.

Source: Northdale Park & Recreation Center, Hillsborough County

How to choose

  • Choose All Persons Rotary Park when documented parking, restrooms, picnic space, and a universal-playground label matter most.
  • Choose Branchton Park when basketball courts and a recently renovated park fit the outing, and you can verify the currently available phase.
  • Choose Northdale when you want the broadest documented mix of outdoor recreation options, while checking each facility's separate schedule.
