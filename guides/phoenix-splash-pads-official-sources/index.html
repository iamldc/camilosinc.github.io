Phoenix Splash Pads: 3 Official City Sources for 2026

Phoenix publishes individual splash-pad pages, but the details are not equally complete. This guide compares three options using only what the City currently confirms. Anything the source does not state stays unknown.

This is not a complete list, and these locations have not been personally tested. The goal is a short, source-backed starting point for checking schedules and choosing the best-documented option for your outing.

1. Altadena Splash Pad

Address: 3711 E Altadena Ave, Phoenix, AZ.

The City lists this location as ADA accessible and documents drinking fountains, a grill/picnic area, a playground, and shade structures.

2026 schedule: The City says standard Phoenix splash pads opened Fridays through Sundays from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. beginning March 20, then moved to seven days a week after May 23. The page does not state an end date.

Unknown on the current page: Restrooms · Parking · Admission cost · Exact splash-pad equipment · Whether every amenity is inside the splash-pad boundary

Before you go: Recheck the City page for the current operating status and confirm that the documented park amenities fit your plan.

Source: Altadena Splash Pad, City of Phoenix

2. Pecos Splash Pad

Address: 17010 S 48th St, Phoenix, AZ.

The City page links this splash pad with Pecos Park, Pecos Pool, Pecos Community Center, and Pecos Skate Park. Those links show nearby City facilities, not proof that every facility shares the same hours, entrance, or availability.

2026 schedule: The same standard schedule is published here: Fridays through Sundays from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. beginning March 20, then seven days a week after May 23. The page does not state an end date.

Unknown on the current page: Restrooms · Parking · Admission cost · Accessibility details · Shade · Seating · Exact splash-pad equipment

Before you go: Check the splash-pad page and any separate facility page you plan to use. Do not assume the pool, community center, skate park, and splash pad operate on one schedule.

Source: Pecos Splash Pad, City of Phoenix

3. Nuestro Splash Pad

Address: 1433 S 9th St, Phoenix, AZ.

Nuestro follows a different seasonal note from the standard Phoenix splash-pad schedule. The City says the splash pad operates from the last Saturday in May through October 1. The surrounding park is listed as open from 5:30 a.m. to 10 p.m.

Unknown on the current page: Daily splash-pad start and stop times · Restrooms · Parking · Admission cost · Accessibility details · Shade · Seating · Exact equipment

Before you go: Do not treat the park's full opening window as proof that the water is running for all of those hours. Confirm the splash pad's same-day operating status with the City.

Source: Nuestro Splash Pad, City of Phoenix

How to choose

  • Choose Altadena when documented shade, a playground, drinking fountains, picnic space, and ADA accessibility matter most.
  • Choose Pecos when you want to investigate several nearby City recreation facilities, and you are willing to verify each one separately.
  • Choose Nuestro when its distinct through-October-1 season fits your date, and you can confirm the water schedule before leaving.

Check the Citywide rules too

Phoenix's Citywide splash-pad page says water features can be turned off during drought and describes closures for sanitation or maintenance. It also lists rules for food, glass, animals, scooters, bicycles, and other items near the splash-pad area.
